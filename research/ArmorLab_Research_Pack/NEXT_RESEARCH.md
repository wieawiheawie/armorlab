# Source notes / next research actions

1. Acquire full WO 194/744, “Firing trials in Tunisia against the hull of a Pz.Kw.VI,” and the complete October 1943 British Tiger reports. The web article gives selected excerpts; shot-by-shot data is needed before quantitative fit.
2. Acquire complete A.F.V.(T) Report No.17 and “Preliminary Report on Armour Quality and Vulnerability of ‘Tiger’ II Turret,” including figures, appendix, hardness traverse and exact angle definitions.
3. Obtain the original Soviet reports at CAMD RF F.328 Op.4867 D.48 L.133 and the original Kubinka NIIBT Tiger II report (final report dated 16 February 1945). The hosted translations are useful leads but are not a machine-readable full set.
4. Read the relevant pages/figures from TM 9-1985-3 and OP 1666 directly, documenting exact 8.8 cm Pzgr.39/1 and 39/43 component dimensions, AP-cap/wind-cap distinction, mass and filler/fuze variants.
5. Locate original Munitionsmerkblatt 5 (OKH 1943) and its penetration-table notes. Reconcile definitions and angle conventions against S07/S09 before accepting any range curve.
6. Locate published test data with both penetrations and stops, or raw repeated-shot data, for comparable rolled homogeneous plate, including shot velocity, plate hardness/chemistry/thickness, obliquity, support and penetration criterion.
7. Exact dynamic stress-strain/failure data for a Tiger I or Tiger II plate sample is not identified in this starter pass. Keep rate-dependent yield, fracture strain, tensile spall and toughness unknown until sourced or explicitly fitted with uncertainty.
8. Make the target CAD geometry independent from material properties: a correct plate thickness and local normal are not a material strength value.
9. Every number going into source code should carry a source ID, exact variant, unit, definition, uncertainty and the date/version of the source record.
